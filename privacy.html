Privacy policy

Version of 20 November 2026. Developer: Nikita Grebņovs, Latvia.

In short: Nolgi does not collect, share or sell your data. The app has no accounts, servers, analytics or ads. The developer sees nothing of what you enter.

What is stored and where. Bills, loans, payments, payee details (IBAN, name, reference), notes and settings are stored only on your device, in an encrypted database. The encryption key is kept in the system's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android).

Permissions. Notifications are used only for the payment reminders you set up. Face ID or your fingerprint is used only to unlock the app and is handled by the system: the app never receives biometric data. The file picker is used only when you choose a backup file to restore.

Backups. You create the backup file yourself. It is encrypted with a password only you know; the password is not stored anywhere and cannot be recovered. You decide where the file goes (iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email, and so on). On iPhone the encrypted database may also be part of your iCloud or computer backup. On Android your data does not move to a new device by itself: use a backup file.

Deletion. Deleting the app removes all data from the device, including the database copies the app makes before an update. You can also delete records in the app.

Contacting support. If you write to us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Messages are kept no longer than needed to reply.

App stores. Downloads and the rating prompt are handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies.

Your rights. Under the GDPR you can ask for access to the data we hold (only support correspondence), its correction or deletion, and complain to the Data State Inspectorate of Latvia (Datu valsts inspekcija, dvi.gov.lv).

Children. The app is not meant for children under 16.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on the app's store page.
