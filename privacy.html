Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

In the free version, Nolgi keeps all your data only on the device, in an encrypted database. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads, and it does not collect, send or share any personal or financial data.

Permissions: notifications are used only for payment reminders you set up; biometrics (fingerprint or face) are used only to unlock the app and are handled by the operating system — the app never sees your biometric data.

The app has no cloud of its own. On Android the data is excluded from system backups. On iPhone the encrypted database may be part of your own iCloud or computer backup and stays encrypted there. Uninstalling the app deletes the data from the device. Rating requests are shown by the app store and follow its own privacy policy.
